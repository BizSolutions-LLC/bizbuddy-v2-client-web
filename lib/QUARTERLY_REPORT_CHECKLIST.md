# Quarterly QuickBooks payroll summary — manual checklist

Use after at least one payroll run is saved for the period (Sunshine Learning 8/27–9/9 is in Q3 2026). Do not use this for 941 Tax Liability or DE-9C — those tabs are disabled placeholders. There is no standalone insurance report; SDI appears as **Employee taxes - CA State Disability Ins** in the Excel file.

1. Open **Payroll → Reports**.
2. Period type **Quarterly**, year **2026**, quarter **Q3 (Jul – Sep)**. Confirm the date range shows `2026-07-01` to `2026-09-30`.
3. Click **Generate Report**.
4. Confirm the orange **Q3 2026 Payroll Report** banner: run count, unique employees, and period net.
5. Click **Export Q3 2026 Report**. Open the downloaded `{COMPANY}_PayrollSummaryByEmployee_{date}.xlsx`.
6. Confirm QuickBooks-style layout: company name, title **Payroll summary by employee report**, columns **Item | Total | employees**, rows including Hours - Regular Pay, Gross pay - Regular Pay, **Employee taxes - CA State Disability Ins**, Net pay, Total payroll cost.
7. Switch period type to **Custom Range**. Confirm the combined period banner is gone and individual saved runs are listed instead.
